Body model and navigation are illustrative, not a medical scan. Male/female body meshes are from Blender's "Human Base Meshes" bundle (Blender Studio and community contributors, via blender.org/download/demo-files; CC0 1.0 — credited here though not legally required). Ovary/HGSOC anatomy and mutation data are drawn from TCGA (2011) and McPherson et al. (Nat Genet, 2016); breast/triple-negative data from TCGA (Nature, 2012), Yates et al. (Cancer Cell, 2017), a population-based metastatic-site study (Gao et al., Precision Medical Sciences, 2023), and a breast cancer subtype metastatic-pattern study (Kennecke et al., J Clin Oncol, 2010); lung/adenocarcinoma data from TCGA (Nature, 2014), TRACERx (Jamal-Hanjani et al., NEJM, 2017; Frankell et al., Nature, 2023), and a population-based metastatic-pattern study (Riihimäki et al., Lung Cancer, 2014); kidney/clear-cell renal cell carcinoma data from Moore et al. (PLOS Genetics, 2011), Gerlinger et al. (NEJM, 2012), TCGA (Nature, 2013), and population-based metastatic-site studies (Dabestani et al., World J Urol, 2016; Bianchi et al., Ann Oncol, 2012); liver/hepatocellular carcinoma data from Nault et al. (Nature Communications, 2013; cross-checked against Schulze et al., Nature Genetics, 2015, TCGA, Nature, 2017, and Aizimuaji et al., World J Gastrointest Oncol, 2025, for cohort-to-cohort variability), Guichard et al. (Nature Genetics, 2012), Laurent-Puig et al. (Gastroenterology, 2001), Friemel et al. (BMC Clinical Pathology, 2016), and population-based metastatic-site studies (Katyal et al., Radiology, 2000; Zhuang et al., Transl Cancer Res, 2025); brain/glioblastoma data from the 2021 WHO CNS tumor classification (Louis et al., Neuro-Oncology, 2021), TCGA (Brennan et al., Cell, 2013), Killela et al. (PNAS, 2013), Snuderl et al. (Cancer Cell, 2011) and Sottoriva et al. (PNAS, 2013) on intratumor spatial heterogeneity, CBTRUS (Price et al., Neuro-Oncology, 2025) for tumor-type shares, and two independent case-series sources (Majd et al., The Oncologist, 2024; Conejero Merchán et al., Open Respiratory Archives, 2026) confirming glioblastoma's extracranial metastasis rate is under 1–2% — not one real patient; prostate/acinar adenocarcinoma data from Cooper et al. (Nat Genet, 2015) and TCGA (Cell, 2015) on multifocal clonal origins and driver mutations, Fontugne et al. (JCI Insight, 2022), Mehra et al. (Cancer Res, 2007), Cyrta et al. (J Pathol, 2022), and Segura-Moreno et al. (Cancer Reports, 2023) on interfocal molecular discordance, Chen et al. (Nat Cancer, 2025) on the SPOP/CHD1 subtype, a large SEER histologic-subtype cohort (Siech et al., Ann Surg Oncol, 2026), and a population-based metastatic-site autopsy study (Bubendorf et al., Hum Pathol, 2000). For all seven cancers, which gene is shown at which site is a simplified teaching device: the site-pattern study establishes where spread/metastasis happens (or, for glioblastoma, where within one tumor mass regional heterogeneity happens instead, and for prostate acinar adenocarcinoma, where independently-arising tumor foci within one gland happen instead) and the genomics study the cohort-level mutation frequencies, not this specific site→gene pairing.
